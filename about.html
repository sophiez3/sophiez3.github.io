02   ABOUT ME

A little about the person behind the projects.

I'm Sophie Zhao, a student at Carnegie Mellon University studying Computational Physics and Computer Science.

I enjoy taking a question apart, finding the underlying mathematical or physical model, and turning it into something I can test. Sometimes that becomes an acoustic physics experiment or numerical simulation; other times, it becomes a full-stack web application powered by AI agents and spatial data pipelines.

I am pursuing technical roles where rigorous analytical thinking and hands-on engineering meet: software engineering, computational physics, quantitative research, and data science.
