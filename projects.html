Gravity Assist N-Body Simulator

PythonSimulationNumerical IntegrationN-Body Dynamics

Simulated orbital mechanics using VPython to model multi-body gravitational interactions and historical planetary gravity assists (Voyager 1 and 2 flybys across Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune). Implemented a 4th-Order Runge-Kutta (RK4) numerical integrator to solve gravitational differential equations across an 8-planet system and the Sun. The simulation dynamically contracts the integration time-step and recalculated visual scale whenever the spacecraft reaches a planet’s Hill sphere. It plots kinetic and potential energies in real time to demonstrate mechanical energy conservation, alongside a sandbox mode for custom planetary orbital configurations and spacecraft launch trajectories.
