Housing Peer Tool — 2026 PNC × 180DC Challenge

Proposed solutions to financial institutions using AI to address housing affordability for the 2026 PNC × 180DC Challenge, accompanied by an interactive Pennsylvania case-study web application.

System Architecture & Methodology

  • Structural Peer Matching & Rent Divergence: Matches a target city to structural peers across population, income, job mix, housing age, commute, broadband, and homeownership, then fits rent against income to quantify divergence and rank key drivers.
  • Explainable Neighborhood Scoring: Scores every tract from 0–1 across four archetypes—vacant sites, transit-adjacent lots, tangled titles and small-landlord rentals, and high energy burden—using threshold-based, explainable weights and peer-tract comparisons.
  • Bank Product Routing & Governance: Routes each neighborhood archetype to a targeted bank product with local partners and KPIs, incorporating human and fair-lending review.
  • Full-Stack & Grounded LLM Pipeline: Built with a FastAPI backend (pandas, NumPy, CSV validation, and an in-memory dataset store), a dependency-free single-page frontend with custom SVG charts, and an OpenRouter-connected LLM generating recommendation memos strictly from computed outputs.
